Sync with the real NFL

Pulls every team's current roster and today's injury report from ESPN, then writes an updated copy of each roster you pick (named …-SYNC-MMDD). Your original roster files are not changed.

  • Players on a different team than in the file move there (trades and signings), joining the depth chart by overall. Practice-squad players are skipped and no team goes over 75.
  • Injured reserve and Out players get injuries with weeks out from the estimated return date. Questionable and Doubtful stay healthy.
  • Custom players (names that aren't on any NFL roster) are left alone.
